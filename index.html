About

I build platforms and backend systems that remain reliable long after the first deployment.

I'm a data and platform engineer focused on designing systems that remain understandable, maintainable, and reliable as they evolve.

Over the past five years I've worked across renewable energy, agribusiness, retail, and risk management, designing and modernizing data platforms on Azure and Databricks. My work has ranged from migrating legacy pipelines and scaling analytics across multiple domains to developing backend services, resolving production incidents, and improving data correctness.

This isn't a collection of tutorials. It's a record of the engineering decisions behind real production systems — the trade-offs considered, the alternatives rejected, the mistakes made, and the measurable outcomes that followed.

If you're interested in why systems succeed or fail over time rather than simply how they're built, you'll probably find something useful here.
